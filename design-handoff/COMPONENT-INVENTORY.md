## How the groups map to the product

| Group | Count | What it is |
|---|---:|---|
| **Public** | 16 | The notice board, a room page, the landlord's public storefront, pricing, how-it-works, legal, the lease templates. Everything a person sees before signing in — and the storefront and room pages are the SEO surface, server-rendered. |
| **Auth** | 8 | Sign in, sign up, the phone-number variants, magic link, password reset, lost number. |
| **Tenant** | 3 | Dashboard, rent, the Renter's Passport. Small because most of a tenant's day is in **Shared** and **Account**. |
| **Landlord** | 9 | Dashboard, the listing wizard, properties and the yard, applicants, services, the public-page editor, verification. |
| **Account (both roles)** | 4 | Messages, notices, settings, closing an account. Deliberately role-neutral: the needs are identical and two copies would drift. |
| **Admin** | 11 | Verification queues, disputes, reports, advertising, referrals, surveys, analytics, user detail. |
| **Shared** | **32** | The largest group by far, and the real design system: task rows, room cards, the portal shell, message threads, the lease-document panel, dialogs, the walkthrough, screen hints. |

⚠️ **Shared is 39% of all components.** That is where a design system lands or
fails to. A change to `app-task-rows` or `app-room-card` reaches both portals at
once; a change to a feature component reaches one screen.

## Components worth opening first

If you are rebuilding the visual language, these carry the most weight:

| Component | Why |
|---|---|
| `app-portal-shell` | The chrome every signed-in screen renders inside. On a phone its sidebar becomes a horizontally scrolling strip, and that strip is the whole navigation for 14 items. |
| `app-task-rows` | One markup definition rendering BOTH portals' "Needs you" lists. The single highest-leverage component in the app. |
| `app-room-card` | The unit of the notice board, and the thing a tenant actually scans. |
| `app-screen-hint` | The per-screen first-use hint. Shipped with its only button white-on-white at 1.00:1 — a reminder that contrast here is not theoretical. |
| `app-navbar` | Public header. Its mobile behaviour has been rebuilt three times and is still the most reported area. |

## What this inventory cannot tell you

- **Which components are actually reachable.** A component existing is not a
  screen anyone can get to. Cross-reference `ROUTE-INVENTORY.md`.
- **Visual weight.** A 600-line component and a 20-line one get one row each.
- **What a component looks like.** There are no screenshots here. The app runs
  locally with `npm start` in `frontend/`; `scripts/mobile-drive.mjs` and
  `scripts/layout-ui-drive.mjs` drive real screens at 360/390/768/1280 if you
  want rendered output rather than source.
